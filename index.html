What the AI numbers mean

Alpha-beta reports its last completed depth and best line. The score is centipawns from the player-to-move perspective; mate uses a large terminal score.

MCTS reports completed simulations and root move statistics. Utility ranges from −1 to +1 for the player at the root. It is a sampled heuristic value, not a win probability.

These scores use different units. Agreement is interesting; disagreement is a position to investigate, not proof that one algorithm is wrong.
